I study medical physics at Salahaddin University in Erbil, and I build software that starts from the Kurdish language: commerce, education and publishing, Sorani first and right-to-left by default. I founded [Kurdistan AI](https://kurdistanai.app).

Right now I'm shipping Reelcart (بازارگە), a store builder for people who already sell on Instagram, TikTok and WhatsApp in Iraq and the Kurdistan Region.
